    第一部分：本节课知识详解 (Lecture 4 Summary)¶

    2.6 Limits of Exponential, Logarithmic & Inverse Functions (指数、对数与反函数极限)¶

    1. 指数函数的极限 (Limits of Exponential Functions)¶

    • 概念与性质 (Properties): 对于底数 \(a > 0\) 且 \(a \neq 1\) 的指数函数 \(f(x) = a^x\)：
    • 直接代入法 (Substitution): 对任意实数 \(c\)，均有 \(\lim_{x \to c} a^x = a^c\)。

    • 趋于无穷时的极限趋势 (Limits at \(\pm\infty\)):

      • 当 \(a > 1\) 时（例如自然底数 \(e \approx 2.718\) 或底数 \(2\)）： $\(\lim_{x \to \infty} a^x = \infty, \quad \lim_{x \to -\infty} a^x = 0\)$
      • 当 \(0 < a < 1\) 时（例如 \(a = 1/2\)）： $\(\lim_{x \to \infty} a^x = 0, \quad \lim_{x \to -\infty} a^x = \infty\)$

    • 核心基本极限 (Core Exponential Limits): $\(\lim_{x \to \infty} e^x = \infty, \quad \lim_{x \to -\infty} e^x = 0\)$ $\(\lim_{x \to \infty} \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^x = 0, \quad \lim_{x \to -\infty} \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^x = \infty\)$

    2. 反函数的极限定理 (Limits for Inverse Functions)¶

    • 定理内容 (Theorem C): 若函数 \(f\) 存在反函数 \(f^{-1}\)，且在 \(x = a\) 处的极限满足： $\(\lim_{x \to a} f(x) = f(a) = c\)$ 则反函数 \(f^{-1}\) 在 \(x = c\) 处的极限满足： $\(\lim_{x \to c} f^{-1}(x) = f^{-1}(c) = a\)$
    • 几何与逻辑直观 (Geometric Intuition): 反函数的图像关于直线 \(y = x\) 对称，定义域与值域互换，因此极限在对应点处保持连续映射关系。例如若 \(\lim_{x \to 5} a^x = 8\)，则必有 \(\lim_{x \to 8} \log_a x = 5\)。

    3. 对数函数的极限 (Limits of Logarithmic Functions)¶

    • 定理内容 (Theorem D): 对于对数函数 \(f(x) = \log_c x\)（底数 \(c > 0, c \neq 1\)）：
    • 内部点极限: 若 \(a > 0\)，则 \(\lim_{x \to a} \log_c x = \log_c a\)。
    • 无穷远处趋势 (当 \(c > 1\) 时): $\(\lim_{x \to \infty} \log_c x = \infty, \quad \lim_{x \to 0^+} \log_c x = -\infty\)$

    🌟 课件应用例题：连续复利投资模型 (Continuous Compounding Model)¶

    • Problem: An investment of \(\$2000\) yields \(8\%\) interest per year compounded continuously. The value of the investment after \(t\) years is given by \(A(t) = 2000 e^{0.08t}\). (a) Find the value of the investment after \(5\) years. (b) Find the time required for the investment to double in value.
    • Process (解题步骤): (a) Evaluate \(A(5)\): $\(A(5) = 2000 e^{0.08 \times 5} = 2000 e^{0.4} \approx \$2983.60\)$ (b) Set \(A(t_0) = 4000\) and solve for \(t_0\): $\(4000 = 2000 e^{0.08 t_0} \implies 2 = e^{0.08 t_0}\)$ Take the natural logarithm on both sides: $\(\ln 2 = \ln\left(e^{0.08 t_0}\right) = 0.08 t_0 \implies t_0 = \frac{\ln 2}{0.08} = \frac{25}{2} \ln 2 \approx 8.66 \text{ years}\)$
    • 中文详解: (a) 5 年后的资金终值直接将 \(t=5\) 代入连续复利公式 \(A(t)\) 计算得到约 \(\$2983.60\)； (b) 投资翻倍意味着终值达到 \(\$4000\)，建立方程后两边取自然对数 \(\ln\)，解得翻倍所需时间 \(t_0 = \frac{25}{2} \ln 2 \approx 8.66\) 年。

    2.7 Continuity of Functions (函数的连续性)¶

    1. 连续性的直观与严格定义 (Definition of Continuity)¶

    • 直观含义 (Intuitive Concept): 在物理与自然界中，连续变化的过程（如一天的气温变化 \(T(t)\)）其图像是一条一笔画出、没有断裂的平滑曲线；而脉冲电压 \(V(t)\) 在开关切换时会发生突变，对应图像上的断点 (Breaks)。
    • 连续三要素测试 (Continuity Test): 函数 \(f(x)\) 在点 \(x = c\) 处连续 (continuous at \(c\))，当且仅当满足以下三个条件：
    • 极限存在: \(\lim_{x \to c} f(x)\) 存在；
    • 函数值有定义: \(f(c)\) 在 \(c\) 处有定义（即 \(c \in D(f)\)）；
    • 极限值等于函数值: \(\lim_{x \to c} f(x) = f(c)\)。

    若上述三个条件中有任何一个不满足，则称函数 \(f(x)\) 在点 \(x = c\) 处不连续 (discontinuous)，\(c\) 称为 \(f(x)\) 的间断点 (Point of Discontinuity)。

    🌟 课件例题 1：补全点使函数连续 (To Make a Function Continuous)¶

    • Problem: Let \(f(x) = \frac{x^2 - 4}{x - 2}\) for \(x \neq 2\). How should \(f\) be defined at \(x = 2\) in order to make it continuous there?
    • Process (解题步骤): Calculate the limit as \(x \to 2\): $\(\lim_{x \to 2} \frac{x^2 - 4}{x - 2} = \lim_{x \to 2} \frac{(x - 2)(x + 2)}{x - 2} = \lim_{x \to 2} (x + 2) = 4\)$ To achieve continuity at \(x = 2\), we must set \(f(2) = \lim_{x \to 2} f(x) = 4\).
    • 中文详解: 原函数在 \(x=2\) 处无定义，但极限存在且等于 4。要使函数在 \(x=2\) 处连续，只需补充定义 \(f(2) = 4\) 即可填补该“无定义洞”。

    2. 间断点的分类 (Classification of Points of Discontinuity)¶

    • 可去间断点 (Removable Discontinuity): 若极限 \(\lim_{x \to c} f(x)\) 存在，但 \(f(c)\) 无定义或 \(f(c) \neq \lim_{x \to c} f(x)\)。可以通过重新定义 \(f(c) = \lim_{x \to c} f(x)\) 来消除间断。
    • 不可去间断点 (Nonremovable Discontinuity): 若极限 \(\lim_{x \to c} f(x)\) 不存在（例如左右极限不相等形成的跳跃间断点，或趋于无穷的无穷间断点）。

    🌟 课件例题 2：间断点的全分析与分类¶

    • Problem: Determine all points of discontinuity of \(f(x) = \frac{\sin x}{x(1 - x)}\), and classify each point as removable or nonremovable.
    • Process (解题步骤):
    • Identify candidate points where the denominator is zero: \(x = 0\) and \(x = 1\).
    • Analyze \(x = 0\): $\(\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin x}{x(1 - x)} = \left(\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin x}{x}\right) \cdot \left(\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{1}{1 - x}\right) = 1 \cdot 1 = 1\)$ Since the limit exists at \(x = 0\), \(x = 0\) is a removable discontinuity.
    • Analyze \(x = 1\): As \(x \to 1^+\), \(\sin 1 > 0\), \(x \to 1\), and \(1 - x \to 0^-\): $\(\lim_{x \to 1^+} \frac{\sin x}{x(1 - x)} = -\infty\)$ As \(x \to 1^-\), \(1 - x \to 0^+\): $\(\lim_{x \to 1^-} \frac{\sin x}{x(1 - x)} = +\infty\)$ Since the limit does not exist (infinite limit), \(x = 1\) is a nonremovable discontinuity.
    • 中文详解:
    • 使分母为 0 的可疑点为 \(x=0\) 和 \(x=1\)。
    • 在 \(x=0\) 处，拆分为第一重要极限 \(\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin x}{x} = 1\) 与连续因子 \(\frac{1}{1-x} \to 1\) 的乘积，极限值为 1 存在，故 \(x=0\) 为可去间断点；
    • 在 \(x=1\) 处，左右极限分别为 \(+\infty\) 与 \(-\infty\)，极限不存在，故 \(x=1\) 为不可去（无穷）间断点。

    3. 常见初等函数的连续性定理 (Continuity of Elementary Functions)¶

    1. 多项式函数 (Polynomials): 在全体实数 \(\mathbb{R}\) 上无条件连续。
    2. 有理函数 (Rational Functions): 在其定义域内（即排除分母为 0 的所有点）处处连续。
    3. 绝对值与根式函数:
    4. 绝对值函数 \(|x|\) 在 \(\mathbb{R}\) 上连续；
    5. 奇次根式 \(x^{1/n}\) (\(n\) 为奇数) 在 \(\mathbb{R}\) 上连续；
    6. 偶次根式 \(x^{1/n}\) (\(n\) 为偶数) 在所有正实数 \((0, \infty)\) 上连续。
    7. 超越初等函数 (Transcendental Functions): 正弦、余弦、正切、余切、正割、余割、反三角函数、指数函数、对数函数在其定义域内部 (Interior of Domain) 均连续。
    8. 初等函数连续性基本定理: 一切初等函数在其自然定义域内都是连续的。

    4. 复合函数的连续性与换元法 (Composite Limit Theorem & Variable Substitution)¶

    • 复合函数极限交换定理 (Theorem E): 若 \(\lim_{x \to c} g(x) = L\)，且函数 \(f\) 在 \(u = L\) 处连续，则： $\(\lim_{x \to c} f(g(x)) = f\left(\lim_{x \to c} g(x)\right) = f(L)\)$ 即：对于连续函数，极限符号与函数作用符号可以自由交换位置。**

    🌟 课件例题 3：复合函数极限与换元计算¶

    • Problem: Evaluate the following limits: (a) \(\lim_{x \to 0} \sin\left(\frac{\sin x}{x}\right)\) (b) \(\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\ln(1+x)}{x}\) (c) \(\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{e^x - 1}{x}\)
    • Process (解题步骤): (a) Since \(\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin x}{x} = 1\) and \(\sin u\) is continuous at \(u = 1\): $\(\lim_{x \to 0} \sin\left(\frac{\sin x}{x}\right) = \sin\left(\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin x}{x}\right) = \sin 1\)$ (b) Using logarithmic properties and the second important limit \(\lim_{x \to 0} (1+x)^{1/x} = e\): $\(\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\ln(1+x)}{x} = \lim_{x \to 0} \ln\left((1+x)^{1/x}\right) = \ln\left(\lim_{x \to 0} (1+x)^{1/x}\right) = \ln e = 1\)$ (c) Let \(u = e^x - 1 \implies e^x = 1+u \implies x = \ln(1+u)\). As \(x \to 0\), \(u \to 0\): $\(\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{e^x - 1}{x} = \lim_{u \to 0} \frac{u}{\ln(1+u)} = \lim_{u \to 0} \frac{1}{\frac{\ln(1+u)}{u}} = \frac{1}{1} = 1\)$
    • 中文详解: (a) 利用外层正弦函数的连续性，将极限符号收入内部，得 \(\sin 1\)； (b) 将分母的 \(\frac{1}{x}\) 提为对数真数的指数，结合第二重要极限 \(\lim_{x \to 0} (1+x)^{1/x} = e\)，对数连续性得 \(\ln e = 1\)； (c) 采用换元法令 \(u = e^x - 1\)，将分子分母转化为 \(\frac{u}{\ln(1+u)}\)，利用 (b) 的结论直接求得极限为 1。

    🌟 课件例题 4：幂指函数 \(f(x)^{g(x)}\) 的连续性与极限¶

    • Problem: Evaluate \(\lim_{x \to 0} (1 + x)^{2/\sin x}\).
    • Process (解题步骤): Rewrite the base and exponent: $\(\lim_{x \to 0} (1 + x)^{2/\sin x} = \lim_{x \to 0} \left[(1 + x)^{1/x}\right]^{\frac{2x}{\sin x}}\)$ Apply the Composite Limit Theorem:
    • Inner limit: \(\lim_{x \to 0} (1 + x)^{1/x} = e\)
    • Exponent limit: \(\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{2x}{\sin x} = 2 \cdot \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x}{\sin x} = 2 \cdot 1 = 2\) Combine the limits: $\(\lim_{x \to 0} (1 + x)^{2/\sin x} = e^2\)$
    • 中文详解: 这是经典的 \(1^\infty\) 型未定式。通过凑配第二重要极限结构 \((1+x)^{1/x}\)，将指数拆分为 \(\frac{2x}{\sin x}\)。利用连续性，底数趋于 \(e\)，指数趋于 \(2\)，最终计算结果为 \(e^2\)。

    5. 区间上的连续性 (Continuity on Intervals)¶

    • 单侧连续性 (One-Sided Continuity):
    • 右连续 (Right Continuous at \(a\)): \(\lim_{x \to a^+} f(x) = f(a)\)；
    • 左连续 (Left Continuous at \(b\)): \(\lim_{x \to b^-} f(x) = f(b)\)。
    • 区间连续性定义:
    • 函数 \(f\) 在开区间 \((a, b)\) 上连续，若它在开区间的每个点处均连续；
    • 函数 \(f\) 在闭区间 \([a, b]\) 上连续，若它在 \((a, b)\) 内处处连续，且在左端点 \(a\) 处右连续，在右端点 \(b\) 处左连续。

    6. 闭区间上连续函数的全局性质：介值定理与零点定理¶

    介值定理 (Intermediate Value Theorem, IVT)¶
    • 定理内容 (Theorem F): 设函数 \(f(x)\) 在闭区间 \([a, b]\) 上连续，且 \(W\) 为介于 \(f(a)\) 与 \(f(b)\) 之间的任意实数。则在开区间 \((a, b)\) 内至少存在一点 \(c\)，使得： $\(f(c) = W\)$
    • 几何意义: 连续函数的图像是一条不间断的曲线。从水平线 \(y = f(a)\) 连到 \(y = f(b)\)，曲线必然会与中间的任意水平线 \(y = W\) 至少相交一次。
    零点定理 (Zero Point Theorem)¶
    • 推论: 若 \(f(x)\) 在 \([a, b]\) 上连续，且端点值异号（即 \(f(a) \cdot f(b) < 0\)），则方程 \(f(x) = 0\) 在 \((a, b)\) 内至少有一个实根 \(c\)。

    🌟 课件例题 5：介值定理证明超越方程根的存在性与二分法¶

    • Problem: Use the Intermediate Value Theorem to show that the equation \(x = \cos x\) has a solution between \(x = 0\) and \(x = \pi/2\).
    • Process (证明步骤):
    • Define a auxiliary function: Let \(f(x) = x - \cos x\), and set \(W = 0\).
    • Check continuity: \(f(x)\) is the difference of two elementary functions (\(y = x\) and \(y = \cos x\)), so \(f(x)\) is continuous on the closed interval \([0, \pi/2]\).
    • Evaluate end points: $\(f(0) = 0 - \cos 0 = -1 < 0\)$ $\(f\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right) = \frac{\pi}{2} - \cos\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right) = \frac{\pi}{2} > 0\)$
    • Apply IVT: Since \(W = 0\) lies strictly between \(f(0) = -1\) and \(f(\pi/2) = \pi/2\), by the Intermediate Value Theorem, there exists at least one \(c \in (0, \pi/2)\) such that \(f(c) = 0\).

    • Conclusion: \(c - \cos c = 0 \implies c = \cos c\), so \(c\) is a solution to \(x = \cos x\).

    • 二分法延伸 (Bisection Method Remark): 进一步考察中点 \(x = \pi/4\)： $\(f\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \frac{\pi}{4} - \cos\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\right) = \frac{\pi}{4} - \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = \frac{\pi - 2\sqrt{2}}{4} \approx \frac{3.1416 - 2.8284}{4} > 0\)$ 由于 \(f(0) < 0\) 且 \(f(\pi/4) > 0\)，根的范围被成功缩小至更精细的区间 \((0, \pi/4)\)。这种不断折半区间逼近零点的方法称为二分法 (Bisection Method)。

    🌟 课件例题 6：不动点定理证明 (Fixed Point Theorem)¶

    • Problem: Prove that if \(f\) is continuous on \([0, 1]\) and satisfies \(0 \le f(x) \le 1\) for all \(x \in [0, 1]\), then there exists a number \(c \in [0, 1]\) such that \(f(c) = c\).
    • Process (证明步骤):
    • Define the auxiliary function \(g(x) = f(x) - x\).
    • Since \(f(x)\) and \(x\) are continuous on \([0, 1]\), \(g(x)\) is continuous on \([0, 1]\).
    • Evaluate \(g(x)\) at the boundaries:
      • \(g(0) = f(0) - 0 = f(0) \ge 0\) (since \(f(x) \ge 0\));
      • \(g(1) = f(1) - 1 \le 0\) (since \(f(x) \le 1\)).
    • Case analysis:
      • If \(g(0) = 0\), then \(f(0) = 0\), so \(c = 0\) is the required number;
      • If \(g(1) = 0\), then \(f(1) = 1\), so \(c = 1\) is the required number;
      • If \(g(0) > 0\) and \(g(1) < 0\), then \(0\) lies between \(g(0)\) and \(g(1)\). By the Zero Point Theorem, there exists some \(c \in (0, 1)\) such that \(g(c) = 0\).

    • Thus \(f(c) - c = 0 \implies f(c) = c\).

    • 中文详解: 构造辅助函数 \(g(x) = f(x) - x\)。利用已知条件 \(0 \le f(x) \le 1\) 推出端点值 \(g(0) \ge 0\) 且 \(g(1) \le 0\)。如果端点取等号则直接得出不动点；若严格异号，则由零点定理可知区间内部必存在 \(c\) 使得 \(g(c) = 0 \implies f(c) = c\)。

    7. 第二重要极限与 \(1^\infty\) 型未定式 (The Second Important Limit)¶

    • 第二重要极限标准型 (Standard Forms): $\(\lim_{x \to \infty} \left(1 + \frac{1}{x}\right)^x = e, \quad \lim_{h \to 0} (1 + h)^{1/h} = e\)$
    • 复合换元推广定理 (Generalization via Variable Substitution):
    • 若 \(\lim_{x \to c} u(x) = \infty\)，则 \(\lim_{x \to c} \left(1 + \frac{1}{u(x)}\right)^{u(x)} = e\)；
    • 若 \(\lim_{x \to c} u(x) = 0\)（且 \(u(x) \neq 0\)），则 \(\lim_{x \to c} \left(1 + u(x)\right)^{1/u(x)} = e\)。

    🌟 课件例题 7：\(1^\infty\) 型极限经典综合例题¶

    • Problem: Evaluate \(\lim_{x \to 0} \left(x + e^{x/3}\right)^{3/x}\).
    • Process (解题步骤):
    • Identify the form: As \(x \to 0\), \(x + e^{x/3} \to 0 + 1 = 1\), and exponent \(\frac{3}{x} \to \infty\). This is a \(1^\infty\) indeterminate form.
    • Let \(u(x) = x + e^{x/3} - 1\). Note that as \(x \to 0\), \(u(x) \to 0\).
    • Express the base as \(1 + u(x)\): $\(\lim_{x \to 0} \left(x + e^{x/3}\right)^{3/x} = \lim_{x \to 0} \left(1 + u(x)\right)^{3/x}\)$
    • Supply the required exponent \(\frac{1}{u(x)}\) for the second important limit: $\(\lim_{x \to 0} \left(1 + u(x)\right)^{3/x} = \lim_{x \to 0} \left[\left(1 + u(x)\right)^{1/u(x)}\right]^{\frac{3 u(x)}{x}}\)$
    • Evaluate the inner limit: $\(\lim_{x \to 0} \left(1 + u(x)\right)^{1/u(x)} = \lim_{u \to 0} (1 + u)^{1/u} = e\)$
    • Evaluate the exponent limit \(\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{3 u(x)}{x}\): $\(\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{3\left(x + e^{x/3} - 1\right)}{x} = 3 \left(\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{x}{x} + \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{e^{x/3} - 1}{x}\right)\)$ Let \(t = \frac{x}{3}\). As \(x \to 0\), \(t \to 0\): $\(\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{e^{x/3} - 1}{x} = \lim_{t \to 0} \frac{e^t - 1}{3t} = \frac{1}{3} \cdot \lim_{t \to 0} \frac{e^t - 1}{t} = \frac{1}{3} \cdot 1 = \frac{1}{3}\)$ So the exponent limit is: $\(3 \left(1 + \frac{1}{3}\right) = 3 + 1 = 4\)$

    • Combine the results: $\(\lim_{x \to 0} \left(x + e^{x/3}\right)^{3/x} = e^4\)$

    • 中文详解: 这是非常经典的 \(1^\infty\) 型未定式。 第一步：将底数写为 \(1 + u(x)\) 的标准形式，其中 \(u(x) = x + e^{x/3} - 1 \to 0\)； 第二步：强制凑出核心指数次方 \(\frac{1}{u(x)}\)，使括号内底数转化为趋于 \(e\) 的结构； 第三步：计算剩余指数部分 \(\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{3 u(x)}{x}\)，将其拆分为 \(3\left(1 + \frac{e^{x/3}-1}{x}\right)\)，利用换元 \(t = x/3\) 计算出指数极限为 \(4\)； 最终求得结果为 \(e^4\)。
